Experiment 2 – Improving Heat Transfer with Extended Surfaces

Steady-state comparison at 15 W. Adjust power to see how temperature and h change.

Newton's Law of Cooling: Q = h · A · ΔT  →  h = Q / (A · ΔT). A larger surface area A means a lower ΔT for the same power, i.e. a cooler surface.
