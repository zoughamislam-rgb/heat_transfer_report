Experiment 3 – Relationship Between Power Input and Surface Temperature

Finned and Pinned surfaces only. Use the sliders to explore how power and temperature relate.

At steady state: Q = h · A · ΔT, so ΔT = Q / (h·A). The plot of Q vs ΔT is linear — its slope equals h·A.
Finned: slope = Q/ΔT ≈ 0.556 W/°C → h = 0.556 / 0.092 ≈ 6.04 W/m²K
Pinned: slope = Q/ΔT ≈ 0.640 W/°C → h = 0.640 / 0.027 ≈ 23.7 W/m²K
