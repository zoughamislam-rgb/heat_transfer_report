# Heat Transfer Simulations

A small academic simulation project developed for the Heat Transfer module at the National Higher School of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (ENSNN).

The repository contains interactive HTML simulations used to explore and communicate several core heat-transfer concepts studied in the course.

## Simulations

- **Transient heat transfer** — visualization of temperature evolution with time.
- **Extended surfaces (fins)** — exploration of heat dissipation through extended surfaces.
- **Power vs. temperature** — study of the relationship between supplied power and thermal response.

## Files

```text
sim_exp1_transient.html
sim_exp2_extended_surfaces.html
sim_exp3_power_vs_temp.html
```

Each simulation is self-contained and can be opened directly in a web browser.

This repository documents part of my academic engineering work and reflects my interest in combining physical modelling, simulation, and clear technical visualization.
